SilenceSwitch Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 3, 2026

Short version: SilenceSwitch stores two settings on your own computer. It never sends any information anywhere. No data ever leaves your device.

What SilenceSwitch does

SilenceSwitch is a Windows system-tray utility. It monitors your audio devices and automatically switches the default audio output back to your preferred device after a configurable period of silence.

Data stored on your device

SilenceSwitch saves a settings file at %APPDATA%\SilenceSwitch\settings.json containing:

  • The name and Windows device ID of your chosen audio output device
  • Your silence-timeout preference (a number of minutes)
  • Whether to switch devices on app startup

This file exists only on your computer. It is never read by anyone other than the app running on your machine.

Data we do not collect

SilenceSwitch collects no personal information. It makes no network connections of any kind. It contains no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising SDKs. It does not access your audio content — it only reads numeric peak levels to detect silence, and those numbers are never stored or transmitted.

Third-party code

SilenceSwitch uses NAudio (MIT licence) to access Windows audio APIs. NAudio makes no network connections and collects no data.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised. Given that the app is intentionally offline-only, no meaningful changes are anticipated.
